# R97 Landing and recovery

Seven high-detail Blender Studio actors receive two original-source-rig-baked morphs: Land (step, sole lowering, weight acceptance) and Brace (retreat, bent-knee absorption, return to idle). Existing upper-body Strike/Recoil remain unchanged. Three semantic clips still ship: Idle/Attack/Hit. The browser viewer permits orbit and scrubbing real GLB animation.

Source-level sole vertex checks confirm zero planted-foot drift during all new poses. Real Chrome desktop+portrait tests confirm Step to Land, Retreat to Brace, reset to Idle and full arena summons. No game rules, scoring, sound defaults or 3D spell systems changed. Roll back to R96 model with `?r97Landing=old`.

These are baked shape key poses, **not** runtime IK, physics collision, root motion or film-grade full-body mocap. Source: Blender Studio / Blender Foundation, CC BY 4.0.
